zh-TW: 匯出 Markdown 時，指向垃圾桶筆記的連結不再壞掉，空的標籤頁不再變成多餘的筆記，並附上一份說明哪些內容沒有匯出；還原備份會帶回名稱特殊的屬性、白板上已移除的卡片不會在還原筆記後跑回來、把筆記移到名稱重複的空間不再失敗。
en: Markdown export no longer leaves broken links to notes in the trash or turns empty tag pages into extra notes, and it now adds a short note on what was left out. Restoring a backup brings back attributes with unusual names, a card removed from a whiteboard no longer returns when its note is restored, and moving a note to a space with a same-named note no longer fails.
