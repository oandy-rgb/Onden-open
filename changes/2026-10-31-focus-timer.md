zh-TW: 新增專注計時：在任務旁按「開始專注」（任務列、任務選單、日子頁，或指令面板），選 15／25／50 分鐘或不計時。手機有一條專注倒數條，電腦是全螢幕，結束時問「做了什麼？」，可以寫進今天的日記或任務下面。
en: Added a focus timer. Start it from a task (its row, its menu, the day page) or the command palette, with 15, 25, 50 minutes or no timer. A bar on the phone and a full screen on the desktop count down, and at the end it asks what you got done, which can go into today's journal or under the task.
