en: The pen no longer needs a tool switch: on handwriting pages, PDFs and whiteboards it draws with the last pen you picked, even with Hand or Select on, while fingers scroll and zoom. The first time a pen is detected, this device switches fingers to scroll-only; to draw with a finger, turn on “Finger draws too” in a page's More menu (Settings › Writing also has “Stylus pen” and “Finger input”). The stylus switch moved from the toolbar into the More menu.
