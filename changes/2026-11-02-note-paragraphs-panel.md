en: The 「Tags」 tab of the info panel now lists this note's paragraphs by topic, and a click jumps to one. 「Turn into a child note」 is now 「Stand alone as its own note」 and 「Expand back into the text」 is 「Take back into the text」: going back and forth keeps a paragraph's task due date, review schedule and links.
