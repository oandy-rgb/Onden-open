zh-TW: 新增「逐步圖」：在筆記輸入「/」選「逐步圖」，用「## 標題」寫每一步，讀的時候可以按 ◀ ▶、用方向鍵或左右滑一步一步看；相鄰兩步的程式碼沒變的行會滑到新位置，新增的行淡入。發布的網站也能這樣看，沒有 JavaScript 時會依序列出每一步，列印時全部展開。
en: New step-by-step figure. Type "/" and choose the step-by-step command, write each step under a "## Title", and read it one step at a time with ◀ ▶, the arrow keys or a swipe. Unchanged code lines slide to their new place and new lines fade in. Published sites play it too; without JavaScript every step is listed, and printing shows them all.
