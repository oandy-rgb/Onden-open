zh-TW: 筆記與標籤頁的分頁（內容｜白板｜表格…）現在是頁面頂端一條滿寬的標題列，永遠靠左、切換分頁時位置不動；每篇筆記的分頁列都有「＋」可新增視角，筆記的 ⋯ 裡不再有「檢視」。白板的「顯示」和「全螢幕」搬到這條標題列的右邊，筆記的 ⋯ 也在同一處；白板全螢幕時標題列還在。手機上改成點筆記頂列的分頁名稱，開出同一張選單切換分頁或新增視角。
en: The tabs of a note or a tag (Content | Canvas | Table…) are now a full-width title bar at the top of the page, always on the left and never moving when you switch tabs. Every note’s tab bar has a “+” to add a view, and the note’s ⋯ no longer has a “Views” section. The board’s “Display” and “Full screen” moved to the right of this bar, with the note’s ⋯; the bar stays in full screen. On a phone, tap the tab name in the note’s top bar to switch tabs or add a view from one sheet.
