zh-TW: 已發布的筆記或標籤改名後，下次發布時網址會跟著新名稱，但舊網址會自動轉到新網址，別人收藏或分享的連結不會失效；下載的網站與 GitHub Pages 也一樣。很長的標題現在得到的網址最多 80 個字。
en: When a published note or tag is renamed, its address follows the new name at the next Publish, and the old address now forwards to the new one, so links people saved or shared keep working, in the downloaded site and on GitHub Pages too. A very long title now gives an address of at most 80 characters.
