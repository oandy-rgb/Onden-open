zh-TW: 在筆記間切換時，游標位置、摺疊的標題與搜尋後的定位都會記在正確的那則筆記上。用「[[」建立新筆記時，若等待期間你已改動那段文字或換了筆記，不會再蓋掉別的字，並會告訴你連結沒有插入。清單的 Tab 縮排也認得「+」開頭的項目。
en: When you switch notes, the caret position, folded headings and the jump after a search now stay with the right note. If you change that text or switch notes while a note created with "[[" is being made, nothing else gets overwritten and you are told the link was not inserted. Tab now also indents list items that start with "+".
