zh-TW: 標籤不再能讓整則筆記變成任務，「可勾選」與「何時做與截止」兩個功能拿掉了。任務頁的任務和筆記裡勾選行後面加「{task}」的任務都照常使用；原本因為標籤而算任務的筆記變回一般筆記，內容不會動。段落選單裡的「把這段做成任務」照舊可用。
en: A tag can no longer turn a whole note into a task, so the "Checkable" and "When and deadline" functions are gone. Tasks on the Tasks page and checkbox lines ending in "{task}" work as before; notes that counted as tasks only because of a tag are ordinary notes again, with their content untouched. The paragraph menu's "Make this block a task" still works.
