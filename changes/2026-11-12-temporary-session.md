zh-TW: 新增「在這台電腦上暫時使用」：借用別人的電腦時，可以在試用版網址最後加上 temp/ 進入，寫的筆記和檔案都不會留在那台電腦上。關掉分頁前按「下載我寫的內容」，下載的檔案只含你這次寫的東西，之後能用「從備份還原」加進自己的 Onden。
en: New "Use it temporarily on this computer": on a computer that is not yours, open the trial at the address ending in temp/, and nothing you write or add stays on that computer. Before you close the tab, press "Download what I wrote"; the file holds only what you wrote this time, and you can add it to your own Onden with "Restore from a backup".
