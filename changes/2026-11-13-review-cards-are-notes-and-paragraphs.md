zh-TW: 複習卡現在只看筆記或段落本身：原本因為帶了複習標籤而成為卡片的筆記與段落都還在，進度也留著。標籤頁不再有「複習」功能；整篇筆記要當卡片，用「把這篇筆記做成複習卡」（已經是卡片時改成「這篇筆記不再當複習卡」），段落照舊寫 {review}，拿掉字就不再是卡片（排程留著，字加回來就接續）。每天複習幾張只剩一個數字，就是「複習狀況」頁的每日上限，不再另有「每天新卡」。
en: Review cards now belong to the note or paragraph itself: everything that was a card through a review tag is still a card, with its schedule. Tag pages no longer have a Review function; make a whole note a card with “Make this note a review card” (it turns into “Stop using this note as a review card” once it is one), and a paragraph with {review} as before. Taking the word out of a paragraph ends the card but keeps the schedule, and putting the word back picks it up again. How much you review in a day is now one number, your daily limit on the Review status page; there is no separate "new cards per day" any more.
