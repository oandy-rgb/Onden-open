zh-TW: 筆記因為標題太長、內容含不能存的字元，或在別的裝置被刪掉而無法送出儲存時，你打的字現在會留在「復原輸入」面板，不會在你打開另一則筆記時消失。另外，儲存中切到別的筆記再切回來，狀態會在存好後正確顯示為已儲存。
en: When a note can't be saved because its title is too long, it has characters that can't be stored, or it was deleted on another device, what you typed now stays in the recovery panel instead of vanishing when you open another note. Switching away and back during a save also now shows “saved” once it finishes.
