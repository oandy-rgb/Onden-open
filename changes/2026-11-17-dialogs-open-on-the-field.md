zh-TW: 打開「新增任務」這類對話框時，游標會直接在輸入欄裡，不用先點一下才能打字，按 Enter 也不會把對話框關掉；平板和手機上，搜尋框（設定、移動到…）與編輯任務不會一打開就跳出鍵盤。標籤頁的「新增欄位」改成先輸入名稱和種類、按「確定」才建立，取消就什麼都不會留下；建立後要設定選項或關聯，請再點一下那個欄位。
en: Dialogs such as "Add a task" now open with the cursor already in the text box, so you can type at once and Enter no longer closes them; on tablets and phones, search boxes (Settings, Move to…) and Edit task do not pop up the keyboard on opening. "Add field" on a tag page now asks for a name and kind first and only creates the field when you press OK; cancelling leaves nothing behind. To set options or a relation on a new field, press the field after it is made.
