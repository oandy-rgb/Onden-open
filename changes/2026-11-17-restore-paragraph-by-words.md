zh-TW: 「救回」已刪除的段落時，會放回它原本前面那一段的後面；前面的段落都不在了，就放在它原本後面那一段的前面；兩邊都找不到才放在筆記最後。是看文字去找，所以筆記上方改過也不會放到別的地方。
en: Restoring a deleted paragraph puts it back after the paragraph that used to precede it; if none of those is left, before the one that used to follow it; only if neither is left, at the end of the note. Paragraphs are found by their words, so edits higher up in the note no longer send it elsewhere.
